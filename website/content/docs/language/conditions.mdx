An `if` statement runs a block when its condition evaluates to `true`. An `else if` tests the next condition, and `else` handles the remaining case.

## Syntax

Parentheses around a condition are required. Conditions must be booleans. Follow the closing parenthesis with `:` and an indented block, or with a brace block. Write `else if`, never `elif`.

## Common mistakes

`if true:` is missing parentheses. `if (1):` has a non-boolean condition. A colon block needs a consistently space-indented body. See [errors](/docs/errors/#indentation) for indentation diagnostics.
