Expressions evaluate to values. Arithmetic requires integers, except `+` also concatenates two strings.

## Syntax

Arithmetic operators are `+`, `-`, `*`, and `/`. Multiplication and division bind more tightly than addition and subtraction. Use parentheses to group expressions. Comparisons include `==`, `!=`, `<`, `<=`, `>`, and `>=`; `not` negates a boolean.

## Updates

Variables support `+=`, `-=`, `*=`, `/=`, and standalone `name++` or `name--` statements. Postfix updates are not expressions.

## Common mistakes

`%`, bitwise operators, `and`, and `or` are not implemented. Use nested conditions for multiple boolean checks. Integer division does not produce a fractional value.
